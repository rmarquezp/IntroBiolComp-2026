# Setup for R sessions

Most operating systems don't ship with an `R` language interpreter. Below are instructions to install `R`, as well as `RStudio`, a popular Integrated Development Environment (IDE) for R. 

## Installing R

### Linux

LIke most software, the easiest way to instal R is directly from the terminal using your standard software manager. On Ubuntu:

```
sudo apt install r-base
```

### Windows and OSX

R is maintained by an organization called "The R Project for Statistical Computing." You can download it from their [website](https://cloud.r-project.org/). Click on the link and then click on `Download R for Windows` or `Download R for macOS` depending on your operating system. In the next page pick the appropriate installer for your operating system version. On Windows pick the `base` version of R. 

An installer will be download it. Click on it and follow the prompts to install R. 

## Installing RStudio

Once you've installed `R`, visit the RStudio [website](https://docs.posit.co/ide/user/#rstudio-ide-oss-downloads) and download the appropriate installer for your operating system. Once it downloads double click it and follow the prompts to install it. On macOSX remember to copy the application to its corresponding folder (usually `Applications`).
